Privacy Policy

Last updated September 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Cheers Social LLC (“we,” “us”) collects when you use the CHEERS! app and this website, how we use it, and the choices you have. The short version: we collect what’s needed to send CHEERS! between you and your friends, we don’t sell your information, and you can delete everything at any time.

What we collect

  • Account information: your email address, password (stored securely, never in plain text), display name, and username.
  • Photos you send: the drink photos you share as CHEERS!.
  • Place names: if you add a location to a CHEERS!, we store the place name you choose, like “Harbor Tap Room.” When you tap “Use my location,” your phone uses your position to suggest a name, but we never receive or store your coordinates.
  • Friends and activity: who you’re friends with, the CHEERS! you send and receive, and when you like or cheer back.
  • Safety information: people you’ve blocked, and any reports you file or that are filed about content you share.
  • Notification token: a code from your phone that lets us send you CHEERS! notifications.
  • Waitlist emails: if you join our waitlist on this website, the email address you enter.

We don’t use advertising trackers, and we don’t access your contacts, camera roll, or camera except when you choose a photo or take one to send.

How we use it

  • To run CHEERS!: create your account, deliver CHEERS! to the friends you choose, send notifications, and show your history.
  • To keep people safe: review reports, enforce our Terms of Use, and prevent abuse.
  • To contact you about your account or important changes, and to tell waitlist members when CHEERS! launches.

Who can see your information

  • Your friends: the people you send a CHEERS! to can see that photo, its place name, and your display name and username. Your photos are private and aren’t visible to anyone you didn’t send them to.
  • Other users: anyone signed in can find you by your exact username to send a friend request, and will see your display name.
  • Service providers: we use Supabase to store data and photos, and Expo and Apple to deliver notifications. They process information only to provide these services to us.
  • Legal reasons: we may share information if required by law, or to protect the safety of our users or the public.

We don’t sell your personal information, and we don’t share it for advertising.

When you use Share to a group chat, the photo and message go to the app and people you choose, outside of CHEERS!.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while you have an account. When you delete your account, we permanently delete your profile, photos, friends list, CHEERS!, and notification tokens. Copies may remain in our encrypted backups for a short time before they’re overwritten. Reports about content may be kept longer when needed for safety or legal reasons.

Your choices

  • Delete your account: in the app, go to History, then Account and settings, then Delete account.
  • Notifications: turn them off anytime in your iPhone’s Settings.
  • Location: adding a place is always optional, and you can turn off location access in your iPhone’s Settings.
  • Blocking: block anyone from the app to stop them contacting you.
  • Access or correction: email us to request a copy of your information or to correct it.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the CCPA or GDPR. Email us and we’ll help.

Security

Your information is encrypted in transit, and access is limited by strict database rules so people can only see what’s meant for them. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Age requirement

CHEERS! is only for people of legal drinking age, and at least 21 in the United States. We don’t knowingly collect information from anyone younger. If you believe someone underage has an account, please tell us and we’ll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we make important changes, we’ll let you know in the app or by email before they take effect.

Contact us

Questions or requests? Email support@cheers-social.com.
